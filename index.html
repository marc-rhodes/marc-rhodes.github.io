About

CTO by day, still writing Apex by night

I'm the Chief Technology Officer at AVL Recruiting Group GmbH in Munich, where I own the full technical stack: custom Salesforce development (Apex, LWC, Experience Cloud), Microsoft 365 and SharePoint administration, front-end development, GDPR compliance architecture, and an AI-powered B2B outreach pipeline running on GCP with OpenAI and Brevo.

Before the CTO role I worked as a freelance Salesforce consultant across recruiting, healthcare (Health Cloud, patient Experience portal), and professional services — which means I've had to think carefully about data security, integration reliability, and making systems that non-technical users can actually trust.

The projects below are anonymised demos of real patterns I use in production. The full implementations are proprietary, but the code here shows the same design decisions.
